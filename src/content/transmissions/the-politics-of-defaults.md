Most systems do not force us to choose. They arrange the conditions under which choosing feels unnecessary.

The default is a quiet form of power. It is the path that appears neutral because it is already paved.

To inspect a system is to inspect its omissions: who is expected, who is exceptional, and what kind of failure has been treated as acceptable.
